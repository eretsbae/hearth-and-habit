We'd love to hear from you — questions about a guide, corrections, or topics you'd like us to cover.

**Email us at [qo5928@gmail.com](mailto:qo5928@gmail.com).** We read every message and usually reply within a few business days.

You can also leave a comment on any article. Comments are read regularly, and questions that need a longer answer often become future guides.

## Corrections

Spotted something inaccurate or out of date? Email us with the article title and the detail you're flagging, or leave a comment on the article — we prioritize correction requests and update articles promptly.

## Topic suggestions

Our guides stay within five core areas: home maintenance, cleaning and organization, energy savings, kitchen habits, and yard basics. If there's a question in one of those areas you can't find a good answer to, tell us — reader questions are where our best guides come from.
