This is the hub for keeping a house working: furnaces and filters, pipes and toilets, gutters, caulk, and the yearly jobs that are easy to forget until something leaks. The guides are written for homeowners and renters in the United States and Canada who would rather spend an hour on prevention than a weekend on repair.

## How to use this section

- **Something is wrong right now?** Start with the troubleshooting guides: a furnace that smells, a toilet that keeps running, pipes that bang, a ceiling stain with no obvious leak. Each one walks from the symptom to the likely cause and tells you plainly when to stop and call someone.
- **Getting ahead of the season?** The winter-prep and seasonal-checklist guides rank the jobs by what they actually prevent, so you can skip the busywork.
- **Deciding DIY versus pro?** We give the cost ranges we could source, the risks that make a job not worth attempting, and what to ask when you do hire out.

## What you can expect from these guides

Every figure comes from a source we link in the article: code bodies, manufacturer manuals, utilities, university extension services, and insurer research. Where the top search results disagree or leave something out, the guide says so and shows the evidence. Guides are reviewed by peter.bae, and each one lists its sources at the end.
