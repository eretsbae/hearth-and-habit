This hub covers the kitchen: food safety, storage, leftovers, and the appliances and tools you use every day. Food safety on this site is always time and temperature, never smell or appearance, and the rules trace to USDA and FDA guidance.

## Start here

If you read one guide in this section, make it [Fridge and Freezer Storage Chart: How Long Food Really Lasts and When to Toss It](https://www.hearth-habit.com/2026/11/fridge-and-freezer-storage-chart-how.html). It puts the federal cold-storage times for leftovers, meat, dairy, and produce in one table, so you can check a container before you reach for anything else in this section.

## How to use this section

- **Is this still safe to eat?** Start with the leftovers, thawing, refreezing, and rice guides. They give the agency rule first and then the cases people actually ask about.
- **Food goes bad too fast:** the fridge and freezer storage chart, plus the greens, bread, and freezer-frost guides, explain where each food should live and how long it keeps.
- **Holidays and gatherings:** how long party food can sit out and how long a frozen turkey takes to thaw in the fridge.

## What you can expect from these guides

Time and temperature limits come from USDA FSIS, FDA, and university extension pages, and the same figure appears in every guide that uses it. Guides are reviewed by peter.bae, and each one lists its sources at the end.
