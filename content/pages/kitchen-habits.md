This hub covers the kitchen: food safety, storage, leftovers, meal planning, and the appliances and tools you use every day. Food safety on this site is always time and temperature, never smell or appearance, and the rules trace to USDA and FDA guidance.

## How to use this section

- **Is this still safe to eat?** Start with the leftovers, thawing, refreezing, and rice guides. They give the agency rule first and then the cases people actually ask about.
- **Food goes bad too fast:** the produce, bread, and onion guides explain where each food should live in the fridge and why the crisper drawer settings matter.
- **Planning and waste:** the Sunday meal plan, the junk-drawer guide, and the storage rules in one place.

## What you can expect from these guides

Time and temperature limits come from USDA FSIS, FDA, and university extension pages, and the same figure appears in every guide that uses it. Guides are reviewed by peter.bae, and each one lists its sources at the end.
