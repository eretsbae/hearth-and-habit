This hub is the outside of the house: lawn problems, fall and winter yard work, gutters, decks, drainage, and pests. The guides aim for the minimum that keeps the yard healthy, with timing drawn from university extension services for your region rather than a single national calendar.

## How to use this section

- **The lawn looks wrong:** start with the symptom guides: brown patches while watering, mushrooms after rain, grass dying along the sidewalk every winter, water pooling in one spot.
- **Seasonal work:** the fall cleanup checklist, raking versus mulching leaves, how late you can seed, and the winter prep that protects faucets and gutters.
- **Structures and drainage:** gutter guards, a deck that feels spongy, grading, and mosquito control that actually works.

## What you can expect from these guides

Timing, seed rates, and plant-care rules come from land-grant university extension pages and are cited in each article. Guides are reviewed by peter.bae, and each one lists its sources at the end.
